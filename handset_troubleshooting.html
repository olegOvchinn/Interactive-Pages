Handset Diagnostics

The admin portal also provides reports for identifying and diagnosing offline or malfunctioning pucks, with detailed status, performance and historical data.

Symptom: Offline pucks/handsets are visually represented on the floor plan as pale/transparent icons, making them easy to identify at a glance.
An offline puck/handset shows as a pale, washed-out icon on the floor plan (circled above) instead of the normal solid colour.
Offline Desks Report
Floorsense Stats & Reports → Floorsense - Per User / Per Desk Spreadsheet Reports.
  1. As an administrator, log into the admin portal and go to Settings, then System Configuration.
  2. On the page that opens, select Stats & Reports, then Floorsense - Per User / Per Desk Spreadsheet Reports.
  3. Look for the “Offline Desks” report — it returns a table of all desks and their current status (online / offline).
The Offline Desks report — download it to see the current online/offline status of every desk.
  1. Resolution — Reconnect Cables: Reconnect all cables connected to the puck/handset. Unplug each cable, then plug it back in firmly, checking for any loose connections or visible damage to the cables or connectors.
Expected Result: The puck/handset reconnects and its icon on the floor plan returns to its normal solid colour, and the Offline Desks report shows it as online.
Escalation Criteria: If the puck/handset still shows offline after reconnecting all cables and checking for damage, tap the Contact support button below and include the site name, desk number, and the Offline Desks report showing the affected desk.

Resolved
