2. Install the Floorsense mobile app

Before you install

Make sure these phone services are switched on:

LocationON
BluetoothON
InternetON — use mobile data or Wi-Fi, whichever is stronger
First launch permissionsAllow Location, Bluetooth and Notifications

Download the app

Return to the Floorsense download page and select the app store for your phone, or download the Floorsense app directly from your app store.

Illustration, not an actual app screenshot

iPhone

Floorsense is supported on iPhone 5, 5S, 6, 6S and later.

Android

Android version 7.1 and later is supported.

Phone only
The Floorsense app works on smartphones. Tablets are not supported.

4. Troubleshooting

Select the issue you're experiencing to get guided steps.

“Account already exists”

Select I already have an account on the registration page and log in.

If you don't remember your password, select Forgot my password and reset it.

Unable to log in

Select Forgot my password below the login window and reset your password.

If you still need help, contact support@smartalock.com.

Can't check in to my desk puck

Are Location and Bluetooth both switched ON for the Floorsense app right now?

Turn on Location and Bluetooth in your phone's Settings, then reopen Floorsense and try checking in again.

The app may not have full access to Bluetooth and/or Location. Try a clean reinstall:

  1. Force-quit the Floorsense app.
  2. Clear its cache/data (Android: App info → Storage → Clear cache/data).
  3. Uninstall the app.
  4. Reinstall it from your app store.
  5. Make sure Location, Bluetooth and Internet are ON before opening it again.
  6. On first run, select Allow for Location, Bluetooth and Notifications when prompted.

App shows a blank page

Has it been less than 3 minutes since you first opened the app, or is your connection unstable?

Allow 1–3 minutes for the floor plan to download, especially on first launch. Use a stable Wi‑Fi or mobile data connection and keep the app open.

Reset the app and let it re-download the floor plan:

  1. Close the Floorsense app completely.
  2. Open your phone's Settings and find the Floorsense app.
  3. Choose the option to reset the app.
  4. Relaunch Floorsense and allow the floor plan to download again.

Where do I get the Setup Code?

The setup code is part of your unique activation link. Open your activation email and copy the part of the link after code=.

Reservations missing from home screen

Refresh the app by touching the top of the screen and dragging your finger down without lifting it (pull-to-refresh).
