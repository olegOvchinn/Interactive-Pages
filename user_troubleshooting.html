Symptom: The Floorsense mobile app won’t open, or stays on its loading screen and never reaches the home screen.
  • ✓ Checked
  • ✓ Checked
  • ✓ Checked
Still not loading? Try a clean reinstall
  1. Force-quit the Floorsense app.
  2. Clear its cache/data (Android: App info → Storage → Clear cache/data).
  3. Uninstall the app.
  4. Reinstall it from your app store.
  5. Make sure Location, Bluetooth and Internet are ON before opening it again.
  6. On first run, select Allow for Location, Bluetooth and Notifications when prompted.
Expected Result: The app opens, downloads the floor plan and shows the Floorsense home screen.
Escalation Criteria: If the app still won’t load after a clean reinstall, tap the Contact support button below and include your phone model, its operating system version and a screenshot of the issue.

Symptom: The app opens, but only a blank white page is shown instead of the floor plan.
  • ✓ Checked
Still blank after waiting? Reset the app
  1. Close the Floorsense app completely.
  2. Open your phone’s Settings and find the Floorsense app.
  3. Choose the option to reset the app.
  4. Relaunch Floorsense and allow the floor plan to download again.
Expected Result: The floor plan downloads and the Floorsense home screen is shown.
Escalation Criteria: If the page is still blank after resetting the app, tap the Contact support button below and include a screenshot of the issue.

Symptom: The phone can’t tap in, check in or confirm a booking — the app doesn’t detect the desk puck, so the desk is not checked in or the booking is not confirmed.
How to check in
  1. Open the Floorsense app.
  2. Go to your pre-booked or preferred desk and put your phone on the desk, close to the handset.
  3. Wait about 15 seconds to allow the phone to communicate with the handset.
  4. If it works, a Confirm / Ignore prompt appears — tap Confirm.
Didn’t work? Troubleshooting
  • ✓ Checked
  • ✓ Checked

Still not working? Try a clean reinstall

The app may not have full access to Bluetooth and/or Location.

  1. Force-quit the Floorsense app.
  2. Clear its cache/data (Android: App info → Storage → Clear cache/data).
  3. Uninstall the app.
  4. Reinstall it from your app store.
  5. On first run, select Allow for Location, Bluetooth and Notifications when prompted, then try to check in again.
Escalation Criteria: If you still can’t tap in, check in or confirm your booking after a clean reinstall, tap the Contact support button below and include the site name, the desk number(s) you tried, whether another desk worked, and a screenshot of the issue.
