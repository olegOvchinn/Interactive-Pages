Desk Analytics Charts View — selecting a desk shows its full usage breakdown, including Total Usage and the individual occupancy ratios.
Find Malfunctioning Sensors
Symptom: Sensors that may require attention will show very few or no readings over a long period of time. A desk stuck at 0% usage, or just as alarming, stuck at 100% usage in the latest report, is a particularly strong signal.
Option 1: Floorplan Heatmap (Desk Analytics)
The Floorplan View heatmap, found under Analytics → Desk Analytics in the admin portal — desk shading shows occupancy, and hovering a desk shows its exact reading.
  1. Open Desk Analytics: As an administrator, log into the admin portal and go to the Analytics tab, then the Desk Analytics submenu. Use the toggle button in the top-right corner to switch from Charts View to Floorplan View.
  2. Select a Floor: As with Charts View, choose the floor to inspect from the left pane.
  3. Choose the Period: Use the Daily, Weekly, or Monthly tabs in the top-left to set the reporting period.
  4. Read the Heatmap: Each desk is shaded by its occupancy percentage. Hover over a desk to update the indicator at the top of the heatmap — this changes both the occupancy percentage shown and the colour intensity, with a darker colour indicating higher occupancy.
Option 2: Desks Motion Sensor Readings Report
Floorsense Stats & Reports → Floorsense - Per User / Per Desk Spreadsheet Reports.
  1. As an administrator, log into the admin portal and go to the Analytics tab, then select the Reports submenu.
  2. Open it via Floorsense Stats & Reports → Floorsense - Per User / Per Desk Spreadsheet Reports.
  3. Search for "Desks Motion Sensor Readings Report".
The report form — set a date range (and optionally a floor), then Download.
Note: There's currently no automatic notification when a sensor needs attention, so it's worth checking this report periodically rather than waiting for a complaint.
Possible Reasons for No Readings
If a sensor has no readings in the report, it could mean:
  • The desk hasn't been used during the period you've selected.
  • The sensor is disconnected or not fully plugged in.
  • The sensor needs to be rebooted (unplug and plug back in).
  • The sensor may be faulty (rare).
  • Readings exist but are consistently too low to count as occupied (less than 50) — the sensor is likely mounted too far from the user and will need to be reinstalled closer.
The audio jack connector on the motion sensor — this is what needs to be fully unplugged and reseated.
  1. Resolution — Reseat the Audio Jack: Unplug the sensor's audio jack completely, then reconnect it all the way in — there should be no visible gap at the connector. Stay at the desk for 3–5 minutes afterward to give the sensor time to reconnect and start reporting again.
  2. Verify: Check the desk Event Log to confirm the sensor is reporting again, or note the desk number and have your Floorsense administrator verify it afterward.
Expected Result: After reseating the audio jack, the sensor resumes reporting readings within a few minutes and the desk event log shows normal activity.
Escalation Criteria: If the sensor still shows few or no readings after reseating the audio jack and waiting the full 3–5 minutes, tap the Contact support button below and include the site name, desk number, and the Desks Motion Sensor Readings Report showing the affected sensor.

Still not loading? Try a clean reinstall
  1. Force-quit the Floorsense app.
  2. Clear its cache/data (Android: App info → Storage → Clear cache/data).
  3. Uninstall the app.
  4. Reinstall it from your app store.
  5. Make sure Location, Bluetooth and Internet are ON before opening it again.
  6. On first run, select Allow for Location, Bluetooth and Notifications when prompted.
Expected Result: The app opens, downloads the floor plan and shows the Floorsense home screen.
Escalation Criteria: If the app still won’t load after a clean reinstall, tap the Contact support button below and include your phone model, its operating system version and a screenshot of the issue.
